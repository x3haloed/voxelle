# Truthful System Construction

Keep the truth. Compress the coordination. Compress the machine.

This skill is the orchestrator for three independently useful disciplines:

- [Causal realization](../causal-realization/SKILL.md): make the capability exist
  as a complete real input-to-output path.
- [Topology compression](../topology-compression/SKILL.md): minimize the
  authorities, representations, boundaries, and coordination edges needed to
  understand and change it.
- [Embodiment compression](../embodiment-compression/SKILL.md): minimize the
  physical machinery and resources needed to execute it.

Read all three specialist skills completely before taking implementation action.
Execute their procedures; do not merely summarize their principles.

## Route narrower work

Use a specialist directly when the request has only one governing objective:

- capability completion, walking skeletons, runtime truth, or “is it really
  done?” -> causal realization;
- duplicated authority, representations, workflows, layers, or coordination ->
  topology compression;
- processes, runtimes, storage, data movement, memory, bandwidth, latency, or
  substrate fitness -> embodiment compression.

Use this orchestrator when construction or change must balance all three.

## Establish one shared contract

Reconcile the specialists into one contract:

- **Capability and envelope:** meaningful behavior and the users, trust, scale,
  data, durability, latency, platforms, deployment, accessibility, integrations,
  and failure conditions for which it must hold.
- **Accepted revisions and exclusions:** explicit tolerances and unclaimed
  adjacent behavior.
- **Causal claims:** semantic, operational, and lived capability, evidence
  horizon, and current risk frontier.
- **Topology preservation:** behavior, contracts, data meaning, authority,
  boundaries, and external obligations that compression must protect.
- **Embodiment depth:** deepest substrate authorized for replacement or
  specialization.
- **Project constraints:** engineering, ownership, portability, dependency,
  operations, schedule, and verification limits kept separate from physical
  fitness.

Write the central truth in one or two causal sentences. If the request omits an
envelope, choose the smallest honest one consistent with it and state the
assumption. Do not inherit the mature scale or architecture of a product merely
because the requested system resembles it.

## Orchestrate the construction loop

### 1. Compare candidate shapes

For greenfield work or a material redesign, compare a small number of candidates
through all three lenses:

- Can it realize the full causal path within the envelope?
- What authorities, representations, boundaries, schedulers, and coordination
  edges does it require?
- What processes, runtimes, storage, data movement, synchronization, bandwidth,
  latency, and operational machinery embody it?

Select the least machinery whose causal path is credible, topology is
intelligible, embodiment depth is authorized, and preservation can be verified.
An uncreated edge is the cheapest edge to remove.

### 2. Realize the risk-bearing slice

Use causal realization to carry one real fact from actual input through the
intended authority and propagation path to actual output. Cross the least-proven
substrate or coordination boundary before broad horizontal construction.

Do not compress hypothetical behavior so aggressively that the capability never
becomes whole. Missing causal edges are missing capability, not removable
machinery.

### 3. Compress the proven shape

Once the slice is observable enough to preserve:

1. Apply topology compression to remove complete duplicate authorities,
   representations, workflows, boundaries, schedulers, and coordination paths.
2. Apply embodiment compression across the authorized depth to remove or replace
   unnecessary runtimes, services, storage, copying, serialization,
   synchronization, and resource consumption.
3. Re-run causal realization evidence after every collapse. A smaller system
   that no longer produces the claimed effect is not truthful.

Topology and embodiment candidates may overlap. Prefer one coherent collapse
whose causal preservation envelope is legible. Do not count engineering effort
as physical cost, and do not treat a physically superior but inadmissible
candidate as the selected project embodiment.

### 4. Expand and recompress

Add the next meaningful feature by extending the existing causal story or naming
a new truth that requires a new path. After it becomes whole, remap all three
dimensions and recompress.

Checkpoint after:

- the first executable vertical slice;
- a new process, persistence, trust, deployment, or substrate boundary;
- asynchronous scheduling, caching, replication, or invalidation;
- the first representative runtime failure at a new seam;
- each meaningful feature family;
- any topology or embodiment collapse;
- the final completion claim.

Runtime and compiler failures are measurements: use them to locate false causal
assumptions, residual topology, substrate mismatches, or hidden machinery.

## Resolve conflicts explicitly

- Capability is invariant unless the contract records an accepted revision.
- Preserve a boundary when it carries named authority, trust, durability,
  failure, scale, ownership, platform, or substrate load.
- Permit required machinery that completes a causal path; challenge it again
  once the path is proven.
- Keep the physically best embodiment distinct from the best project-admissible
  embodiment.
- If semantic meaning is missing, stop that lane and identify its authority and
  needed evidence. If work is merely large or unclear, investigate or choose a
  smaller complete slice rather than deferring it indefinitely.

## Unified fixed point

Stop within the scoped evidence and authorized depth only when:

1. Every claimed capability has a verified real causal path.
2. Every decision has one intelligible authority unless distribution is itself
   required.
3. Every representation and boundary carries distinct meaning or substrate load.
4. Every cross-boundary edge and scheduler represents necessary explicit
   coordination.
5. No evidence-supported topology collapse materially simplifies change without
   violating the contract.
6. No admissible embodiment candidate materially reduces physical machinery
   while preserving capability.
7. Semantic, operational, and lived evidence is proportional to the claims.

State the evidence horizon, bounded gaps, physically deeper candidates stopped
by the contract, and next risk frontier. This is a local fixed point, not a claim
of global optimality.

## Candidate shapes
[Causal viability, topology, embodiment, selected shape, and rejected candidates]

## Realization
[The real input-to-output slice and semantic/operational/lived evidence]

## Compression
[Topology removed, embodiment removed, surviving authorities and machinery]

## Unified verification
