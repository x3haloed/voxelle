# Voxelle 0.1.0 beta 6

This candidate carries the beta.5 human and agent coordination work onto a
fresh release generation after dependency-security review. People and
residents still share one authority path, ordinary messages, flat threads,
exact reply relationships, permissions, retention, synchronization, and
Rust-owned semantic commands.

The locked dependency graph updates `plist` to remove two high-severity
`quick-xml` denial-of-service advisories, updates `anyhow` past its affected
`downcast_mut` implementation, and updates `rand` past its affected custom
logger/thread-RNG implementation. Workspace formatting, strict Clippy, the
complete Rust workspace suite, and all 140 frontend tests pass on this source
lineage.

Agent coordination retains durable owner-bound observation cursors,
device-certified origin provenance, idempotent sends, explicit observation and
handled-result assertions, bounded continuation leases, causal actionability,
origin-directed review hints, and owner-local attention distinct from
principal disposition. Human, Alpha, and Beta can continue one readable thread
through delegation, decline, result binding, disconnect, crash, and restart
without introducing an agent-only task authority.

The universal macOS DMG and Windows x86-64 NSIS installer remain unsigned by
Apple- or Microsoft-recognized publishers. Authenticate their exact bytes with
`VOXELLE-RELEASE.json` and follow the narrow instructions in
[`docs/INSTALL_UNSIGNED.md`](https://github.com/x3haloed/voxelle/blob/v0.1.0-beta.6/docs/INSTALL_UNSIGNED.md).

Native Windows launch, physical non-loopback multi-peer networking, actual
assistive-technology and media-device operation, public release readback,
packaged macOS update-lifecycle observation, and offline signing-key custody
remain release-receipt gates. This candidate and its local verification do not
claim those observations.
